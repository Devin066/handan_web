# antislop audit 001 (2026-09-23)

Scope: frontend under `src/` (pages, components, theme). Direction source: `DESIGN.md` (data-dense operational dashboard, navy primary, amber = attention only, light only).

Design Read: internal manufacturing ERP for operators reconciling numbers, dense-dashboard language, dial ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1 (calm is the stated direction, not a fallback).

Priority: HIGH = Hard Gate, MEDIUM = Purpose-Gate, LOW = Quality Lock.

## HIGH

1. **Dead nav: Settings > Members** (`_menu.tsx`, `avatar-dropdown.tsx`) points to `/system/users`, which has no page (404). R-24, R-26.
2. **Placeholder stats pages in the nav** (`pages/selling/stats.tsx`, `purchasing/stats.tsx`) render a bare "Sales Order Stats" div. Either remove from menu or label "Coming soon". R-24, R-38.
3. **Notification bell is a dead control** (`header-actions.tsx`): backed by a permanently empty `mockNotifications` array. R-26, R-38.
4. **Header icons not keyboard reachable** (`header-actions.tsx`): bell and quick-actions are bare icons, no button role, no focus, no accessible name. R-32.
5. **Menu items use `<div onClick>` + `router.push`** (`layout.tsx` `menuItemRender`): no real link, so no middle-click / open-in-new-tab, and keyboard activation depends on the Menu wrapper. Use `next/link`. R-32.
6. **Dashboard has no error state** (`dashboard/index.tsx`): if any query fails the KPIs show `$0.00` / `0` as if real. Also `receiptNotes.loading` is left out of `loading`. R-27, R-17.
7. **Login copy and behaviour** (`login/index.tsx`): "pls input your email", password placeholder `123123123`, "login success", and `message` + `router.push` fired during render (runs on every re-render). R-26, R-16 copy quality.
8. **Detail drawers fixed at `width="60%"`** (bom, purchase-order, sales-order, work-order, receipt-note, delivery-note): about 225px wide on a phone. R-03.
9. **Low-contrast missing-value dash** (`customer/index.tsx`): `#94A3B8` on white is about 2.6:1. R-25.
10. **Em dash in UI copy** (`configuration/index.tsx` body text and select labels, `customer/new.tsx` collapse label). R-02.
11. **Em dash as the missing-value glyph** (`utils/format.tsx`, `customer/index.tsx`): `DESIGN.md` explicitly specifies `—` for missing values. This collides with R-02; owner decision needed: keep (data glyph, noted override) or switch to an en dash `–`.

## MEDIUM

12. **Rainbow quick-actions** (`header-actions.tsx`): each action has its own antd-default colour (`#1890ff`, `#52c41a`, `#722ed1`), outside the palette. R-29, R-01.
13. **Dashboard KPI icons in four colours**, and Receivable uses amber, which `DESIGN.md` reserves for "needs attention". R-29, R-31.
14. **Stray hardcoded colours bypassing `theme.tsx`**: beige `#ab956d` totals (sales/purchase-order new), antd blue `#1677ff` / `#ff4d4f` link buttons (bom, item, order-item-form), `#f7f8fa` drawer backgrounds, `#fafafa` loader, `#999`/`#d9d9d9` in header. Breaks the "components carry no colours" rule in `DESIGN.md`. R-29, R-20.
15. **Duplicate Help**: help link in the header and a floating Help button, plus a floating back-to-top on screens whose tables already scroll in place. Floating group overlaps table pagination on the right edge. R-31, C-3.

## LOW

16. **Sign-out toast fires before sign-out completes** (`avatar-dropdown.tsx`); "Sign Out" vs "Sign in" casing inconsistent. C-1.
17. **Public `/sandbox` page** ships a lone "sandbox" button. C-3.
18. **Landing module cards all identical** (`pages/index.tsx`), plus a tech-stack line that tells operators nothing. Acceptable for a sign-in splash; optional. R-14, C-3.

## Clean

- Tokens, focus ring, reduced-motion, tabular figures, `DataTable` empty state: solid and match `DESIGN.md`.
- No gradients, glass, glow, fake stats, testimonials, FAQ, or buzzwords.
- Light-only is a documented, coherent decision (R-21 satisfied by reason).
